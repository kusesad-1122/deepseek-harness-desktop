## 本版本重点

### 1. 记忆与知识库统一存储（SQLite 统一事实层）

- 新增 `node:sqlite`（`WAL`）统一库 `unified.db`：`events`（可追溯日志）→ `candidates`（模型待审）→ `facts`（已批准 `MEMORY.md/USER.md` 条目）→ `knowledge_cards`（结构化知识）→ `document_chunks` → `relations/audit` + `FTS5`（`fts_facts/fts_cards/fts_chunks` `unicode61`）
- 旧文件兼容迁移：首次启动从 `MEMORY.md/USER.md/knowledge.json/CONVERSATIONS.md` 幂等导入，之后 `MEMORY.md/USER.md` 为只读投影（`facts`→`§` 回写），`45d` 内可回滚
- 生命周期：模型只产 `candidate`，`writeApproval` 审后落 `fact`，`pending→approved/superseded/rejected` 可审计，`budget` 按 `memoryCharLimit/userCharLimit` 强校验
- 混合检索：`FTS5 BM25 OR LIKE/bigram`（CJK 双字）+ `tags4/title2/summary1` 权重，`8` 条封顶；`retrieveTopK=4` 空查询回退最新；文档块增量索引（`hash` 变化才重切 `800/100`）
- 知识页/关系图为可重建投影：`buildKnowledgePages` 按标签聚合、`buildRelations` 按共享标签权重构边，均可在 `facts` 上重算

### 2. 文档邻域与知识页

- `document-indexer` 支持 `.md/.txt` 等 `512KiB` 内文件，`chunkText` 段落感知 + `100` 重叠，保持标题-段落邻域
- 新增路由 `POST /store/document/index`（单文件/目录 `maxFiles 200`）、`GET /store/document/search?q=&limit=`、`GET /knowledge/pages`、`GET /store/graph`

### 3. 统一存储面板

- 扩展面板新增 `DB 统一存储` 页：统计 `facts/cards/chunks/pending`、待审队列一键批准/拒绝（同步回写 `MEMORY.md`）、知识页预览、`FTS5+CJK` 检索框、关系图 `12` 边预览
- 桥接：`memory/knowledge` 工具与 `systemPrompt` 注入优先走 `unified` 检索，失败回退原内存索引；`mountStoreRoutes` 复用同源 `4KiB` 帽

### 4. 修复 Office Desk 常驻高占用与切换卡顿

- Office Desk 背景 WebGL 极光场原为 `requestAnimationFrame` 60fps 常驻，即使切到原生对话（iframe `display:none`）或窗口隐藏仍持续 `gl.drawArrays`，导致 50% CPU/GPU 占用与切对话卡死
- 现改为可见性感知：隐藏/切对话/`document.hidden`/`prefers-reduced-motion` 时暂停，仅 1fps 轮询；恢复时自动续跑；DPR 上限 2→1.5，`resize` 节流 120ms
- 兼容层 `setVisible` 同步 `postMessage {type:'dshWebglVisibility'}` 控制 iframe 内暂停/恢复

### 5. 兼容层刷新去重

- `refreshAll()`（`memory/knowledge/news` 并行拉取 + 按页渲染）增加 `refreshing/refreshQueued` 去重，快速切页不再并发堆积导致界面卡顿

## English

**What's new in 2.0.27**

- **Unified SQLite fact layer** (`unified.db` `WAL` + `FTS5` `unicode61`): `events` → `candidates` (pending) → `facts` (`MEMORY.md/USER.md`) → `knowledge_cards` → `document_chunks` → `relations/audit`; one-shot migration from `MEMORY.md/USER.md/knowledge.json` with `§` projection, `budget` enforced, `FTS5 BM25 OR CJK bigram` hybrid (`tags4/title2/summary1`, top 8).
- **Document neighbourhood & knowledge pages**: `512KiB` chunker (`800/100`), `POST /store/document/index`, `GET /store/document/search` `GET /knowledge/pages` `GET /store/graph` (shared-tag weighted edges, rebuildable from facts).
- **Unified Store panel**: `DB` tab shows `facts/cards/chunks/pending`, approve/reject queue (writes back to `MEMORY.md`), page preview, `FTS5+CJK` search, `12`-edge graph.
- Office Desk WebGL aurora now pauses when hidden/offscreen (native conversation visible, document hidden, or reduced-motion), polling at 1fps instead of 60fps; resumes automatically when visible. DPR capped 1.5 and resize debounced 120ms, fixing 50% CPU/GPU hang when switching conversations.
- Compatibility layer `refreshAll()` de-duplicated to avoid concurrent fetches on rapid page switches.

**Known issues**

- Windows x64 only; installer is unsigned.
