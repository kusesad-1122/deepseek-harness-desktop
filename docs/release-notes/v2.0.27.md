## 本版本重点

### 1. 修复 Office Desk 常驻高占用与切换卡顿

- Office Desk 背景 WebGL 极光场原为 `requestAnimationFrame` 60fps 常驻，即使切到原生对话（iframe `display:none`）或窗口隐藏仍持续 `gl.drawArrays`，导致 50% CPU/GPU 占用与切对话卡死
- 现改为可见性感知：隐藏/切对话/`document.hidden`/`prefers-reduced-motion` 时暂停，仅 1fps 轮询；恢复时自动续跑；DPR 上限 2→1.5，`resize` 节流 120ms
- 兼容层 `setVisible` 同步 `postMessage {type:'dshWebglVisibility'}` 控制 iframe 内暂停/恢复

### 2. 兼容层刷新去重

- `refreshAll()`（`memory/knowledge/news` 并行拉取 + 按页渲染）增加 `refreshing/refreshQueued` 去重，快速切页不再并发堆积导致界面卡顿

### 变更摘要

- 版本 2.0.26 → **2.0.27**
- `fix(desktop): 修复 Office Desk WebGL 常驻高占用与兼容层重复刷新`
- `feat/fix` 延续 2.0.26 的办公工作台整合与扩展面板修复

## English

**What's new in 2.0.27**

- Office Desk WebGL aurora now pauses when hidden/offscreen (native conversation visible, document hidden, or reduced-motion), polling at 1fps instead of 60fps; resumes automatically when visible. DPR capped 1.5 and resize debounced 120ms, fixing 50% CPU/GPU hang when switching conversations.
- Compatibility layer `refreshAll()` de-duplicated to avoid concurrent fetches on rapid page switches.

**Known issues**

- Windows x64 only; installer is unsigned.
